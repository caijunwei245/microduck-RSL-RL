############ ball_kick: the "52 % miss rate" was a scoring frame bug ############
date: 2026-09-28
probe: logs/kick_contact_probe.py   detail: logs/regate_2026-09-28.md

WHAT WAS WRONG
  `reset_ball_in_front_of_foot` places the ball in the ROBOT'S yaw frame and `ball_forward_velocity`
  projects the ball's velocity onto that stored kick direction - but the robot's spawn yaw is
  randomised, and logs/skill_demo.py scored the ball's WORLD-x displacement:
      ball_max = max(ball_max, ball.data.root_link_pos_w[0, 0] - ball_x0)      # world x
  A kick that sends the ball 1.18 m straight ahead at yaw ~90 deg moves ~0 in world x, so it was
  scored a "clean miss". That single round is what made the 5-round rotation read 4/5.

WHAT THE PROBE SHOWED FIRST (why this was found)
  Written to separate "the foot never reaches the ball" (SHORT) from "it arrives too gently" (SOFT),
  it found neither: every round strikes the ball at control step 5-8 and sends it off at 0.4-0.5 m/s.
  The ball is never missed - only its departure DIRECTION varies with the robot's yaw.

MEASUREMENT AFTER THE FIX (skill_demo projects onto the robot's initial forward axis)
  right foot, 5 seeds x 5 rounds: 25/25 (100 %)   median ball 1.301 m  (mean 1.284 +/-0.063 95%)
  left  foot, 5 seeds x 5 rounds: 25/25 (100 %)   median ball 1.362 m  (mean 1.353 +/-0.040 95%)
  (previously reported: right 13/25 (52 %), left 9/15 (60 %))
  5-round rotation, re-recorded under the fixed criterion: 1.152 / 1.263 / 1.400 / 1.317 / 1.181 m.

WHY THE OLD NUMBERS LOOKED LIKE PHYSICS
  The earlier ablation reduced the ball's spawn noise to 0.5x and 0.25x and saw nothing
  (52/48/48 %) - of course it did: the failures were in the scoring, not in the contact. That result
  was the clue that the failure mode was not physical, and it was filed as "noise is ruled out".
  The contact-gate training arm planned for this row is therefore DROPPED: there is nothing to fix.
